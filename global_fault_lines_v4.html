Sources & method
  • Forecasts: VIEWS (Uppsala University / PRIO). Monthly, country-level predictions of state-based battle deaths, 1–36 months ahead. The page loads the newest run automatically.
  • News momentum & headlines: GDELT DOC 2.0. Share of global coverage matching each region's conflict query, updated every 15 min.
  • Crowd forecasts: Polymarket. Live real-money prediction-market odds, where a relevant market exists.
  • Context: editorial summaries and the ACLED 2026 Watchlist. These are static.

The outlook labels and the prep alarm come from simple threshold rules on these feeds, explained on each card. They are signals, not predictions of specific events, and never an official warning.
